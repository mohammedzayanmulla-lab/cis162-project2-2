Tactical Learning Guide

Expand each section below to learn the key tactical responsibilities of every position on the soccer field.


Forwards should constantly create space, make runs behind defenders, press the opposing back line, finish chances inside the penalty area, and work with teammates to create scoring opportunities.

Midfielders connect the defense and attack by scanning the field, maintaining possession, creating passing lanes, controlling the tempo, and supporting teammates on both ends of the field.

Defenders should stay organized, communicate with teammates, mark opponents, win tackles, intercept passes, and begin attacks by making smart passes from the back.

Goalkeepers organize the defense, communicate constantly, position themselves correctly, distribute the ball effectively, and make saves that prevent scoring opportunities.
